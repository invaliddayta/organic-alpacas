# Phase I (AI-aided) – figure log

Every candidate figure was rendered to PNG, opened by the agent as an image and critiqued against: one question per figure,
readable in about 20 s at 1080p, colour-blind safe palette (Okabe-Ito, viridis/magma), honest axes (bars from 0, CIs where
counts are small), a title that states the finding, no redundancy with another figure. Drafts lived in a scratch folder and
are not committed; only the final versions are in `phase1/ai-aided/figures/`.
